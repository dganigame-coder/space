Black Hole Jets & Limb Brightening

Event Horizon Telescope imagery of Centaurus A reveals relativistic jets whose outer edges ("limbs") shine intensely while their central spine remains dim.

MHD & Synchrotron Mechanism: Relativistic electrons spiral along curved helical magnetic fields. When pitch angles become anisotropic near the jet boundary, forward-beamed synchrotron radiation aligns with our line of sight along the edges.

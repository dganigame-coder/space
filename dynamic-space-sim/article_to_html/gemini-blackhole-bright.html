Relativistic Jet Limb Brightening

Observations of Centaurus A reveal that the outer edges ("limbs") of black hole jets glow far brighter than their central spine.

Physics Mechanism: Electrons spiral along curved helical magnetic fields. When electron pitch angles are anisotropically aligned near the jet boundary, their forward-beamed synchrotron radiation aligns with our line of sight along the edges.

Welopōʻai

28 Light-Years Away

Hierarchical Triple-Star System • 1 Red Dwarf & 2 White Dwarfs

Named via ‘Imiloa’s A Hua He Inoa program (welo = inherited trait, pōʻai = orbit), Welopōʻai is the closest known triple system with two white dwarfs. SPIRou radial velocity shifts (~16 km/s) and Gemini North ‘Alopeke imaging revealed a massive close white dwarf (<1% mass uncertainty) that remarkably spared its red dwarf companion during its giant phase.

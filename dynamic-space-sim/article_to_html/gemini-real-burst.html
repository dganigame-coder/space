Solar System & Earth

You are viewing Earth and the inner Solar System. Earth orbits our Sun at ~1 Astronomical Unit (AU).

When extreme cosmic phenomena like Gamma-Ray Bursts (GRBs) occur in distant galaxies, their sheer energy output is staggering—yet distance and geometry ensure complete safety for our planet.

Why Earth was safe:
  • Vast Distance: ~130–140 million light-years away.
  • Inverse-Square Law: Radiation intensity drops exponentially with distance ($1/d^2$).
  • Beaming Angles: Narrow relativistic jets were either off-axis or directed away from Earth's line of sight.

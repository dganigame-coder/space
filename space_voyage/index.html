To the Edge
of the Observable Universe

Lift off from Earth. Dive past the Sun, through Saturn’s rings and out beyond the heliopause. Then on through 13 real exoplanet systems from NASA’s Exoplanet Archive, from the lava world 55 Cancri e to Kepler-16’s two suns and the pulsar planets. After that come the Orion Nebula and Sagittarius A*, and the last leg crosses the cosmic web to the light of the Big Bang, 46.5 billion light-years away. Take the controls at any moment and fly it yourself: gravity, heat, cold, tides and radiation all act on your ship.
